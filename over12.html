Dinghies Over 12 Feet

Larger dinghies offer increased stability, speed, and capacity for two sailors. They're ideal for racing, day sailing, and light coastal cruising.

ILCA Dinghy (Laser)

Length: 13' 10" (4.23 m)

Use: Olympic racing, solo performance

The standard for one-design racing worldwide. Demands fitness and precision.

RS Venture

Length: 12' 6" (3.81 m)

Use: Training, family sailing, adaptive sailing

Wide, stable, and highly versatile. Features a simple rig and optional trapeze.

Wayfarer

Length: 15' 9" (4.80 m)

Use: Cruising, expedition sailing

A legendary open boat capable of coastal passages. Features a sliding gunter rig and ample storage.
